# Mockups

## Overview
The system includes a set of mockup concepts for enterprise dashboards, brochure layouts, and service presentation modules. These mockups define the visual and functional structure of the company’s digital and print assets.

## 1. Command Center Dashboard
### Layout
- left panel: dispatch feed and service statuses
- center panel: GPS map and route intelligence
- right panel: technician and asset tracking
- top bar: system health, alerts, and service KPIs

### Visual Style
- dark operating interface
- blue and orange accent colors
- status chips for service severity
- route lines and geolocation markers

## 2. Dual-Monitor Operations View
### Monitor 1
- issue queue
- vehicle details
- service state
- repair team assignments

### Monitor 2
- fleet map
- GPS trails
- technician live positions
- route ETA and service coverage

## 3. Tri-Fold Brochure Concept
### Front Cover
- truck image or clean heavy-duty transport silhouette
- company name and tagline
- brand colors

### Internal Panels
- services overview
- why choose us
- after-hours support
- customer trust and uptime positioning

## 4. Enterprise Rollout Slide
### Content
- title: “EH Graced Roads Solutions Operational Rollout”
- summary of dispatch, repair coordination, and GPS visibility
- clean corporate presentation design
- professional heavy-duty fleet context

## 5. Fleet Health Panel
Includes:
- unit status indicators
- readiness and service queue
- technician availability
- route and ETA estimates
- repair escalation notes

## 6. Driver and Technician View
- mobile-friendly state summary
- assigned route and status
- service checkpoints
- estimated arrival and repair progress

## Design Standards
- polished, enterprise-grade presentation
- operational clarity above decorative complexity
- strong readability for dashboard and service workflows
- consistent use of brand palette and messaging
