# System Rules

## Mandatory Company Rules
1. No tow service or winching service is ever allowed.
2. All workflows must prioritize repair coordination.
3. All customer-facing language must reflect repair-first operations.
4. All AI output must remain aligned to branding and company positioning.
5. Real-time systems must support dispatch visibility and route transparency.
6. After-hours assistance must route to repair services, not towing.
7. Emergency escalation must always prefer safe, repair-centered resolution.

## AI and Copilot Standards
- Treat this document as the source of truth for the operating model.
- Keep outputs consistent with company messaging and technical design.
- Maintain a professional, enterprise-grade tone.
- Do not generate tow-related features, language, or offers.
- Use structured, operationally accurate documentation.

## Operational Constraints
- heavy-duty repair coordination only
- no tow yard routing
- no winch or recovery services as a primary path
- route technicians and service partners to repair support
- maintain system health dashboards for visibility

## Quality Standards
- accurate issue diagnosis
- clear dispatch communication
- strong service continuity logic
- safety-first operations
- visibility across fleet, GPS, and technician systems
