# Service Policy: No Towing or Winching

EH Graced Roads Solutions LLC does not provide towing, winching, vehicle recovery,
heavy recovery, or off-road recovery services.

The dispatch automation engine and live breakdown intake reject requests containing
these prohibited services. They are not dispatched, billed, or saved as Grace
service records. Grace provides commercial roadside diagnostics, minor repair, and
other explicitly authorized roadside services only.
